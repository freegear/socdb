Evatronix IP Cores

Our cores may grouped into the following categories:

  • 8051-based microcontroller cores;
  • Z80-based microprocessors and related peripherals;
  • other 8-bit mircoprocessors / microcontrollers;
  • serial bus interfaces;
  • data communication & networking;
  • 16-bit microprocessors & digital signal processors.
  • Numeric coprocessor cores

Evatronix provides its customers with the soft & firm IP cores (virtual components) to enable them cut time-to-market for their system-on-chip designs. Our cores are developed according to the reusability rules and guidelines published by Michael Keating (Synopsys, Inc.) and Pierre Bricaud (Mentor Graphics Corp.) in their Reuse Methodology Manual.Its quality is measured by OpenMORE questionnaire and is above of 90% of this measure for all cores.Deliverables for the cores include:

  • VHDL source code (Verilog versions for most of the cores are available too,
  • testbench & extensive testsuite,
  • datasheet
  • user guide

We offer also firm versions of the cores for Altera and Xilinx technologies. A netlist description for Altera or Xilinx devices instead of the RTL source code is then delivered to the customer (at lower cost). Some of our cores are available through Xilinx AllianceCORE and Altera AMPP programs.
